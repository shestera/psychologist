# Methods, Formulation, and Record Structures

## Decisions supported

- Keep CBT, ACT, motivational interviewing, behavioral activation,
  emotion- and attachment-informed cycle work, and relapse prevention as
  bounded method families rather than treatment manuals.
- Use formulation as a revisable organizing map, not proof of cause or a
  treatment outcome.
- Make exercises collaborative and optional, with rationale, burden, and stop
  conditions.
- Track user-defined outcomes proportionately rather than turning measurement
  into the purpose of the conversation.
- Adapt SOAP, 5P, PICO or PECO, GRADE-informed language, and SBAR cautiously,
  while explicitly preventing clinical-authority leakage.

## Method provenance

- NICE describes structured CBT and behavioral activation, shared choice,
  outcome review, and therapy-specific competence:
  [NICE adult depression guideline](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/recommendations).
- The Association for Contextual Behavioral Science describes the six ACT
  processes used as the project's conceptual map:
  [ACT Video Series: Six Core Processes](https://contextualscience.org/act_video_series_six_core_processes_act).
- SAMHSA TIP 35 describes motivational interviewing as a counseling style:
  [Enhancing Motivation for Change](https://www.samhsa.gov/resource/ebp/tip-35-enhancing-motivation-change-substance-use-disorder-treatment).
- ICEEFT provides model-developer provenance for emotionally focused therapy;
  it does not establish fit or effectiveness for a particular user:
  [ICEEFT](https://iceeft.com/).

## Session, formulation, and monitoring evidence

- Goal consensus and collaboration have moderate associations with
  psychotherapy outcome, without establishing collaboration alone as causal:
  [Tryon et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30335451/).
- NICE shared-decision guidance supports agreeing priorities and available
  time, connecting options to goals, discussing benefits, risks, alternatives,
  no treatment or no change, and stating decisions and review points:
  [NICE NG197](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng197/chapter/recommendations).
- Case-formulation reviews find limited evidence that formulation itself
  improves outcomes:
  [Easden and Kazantzis, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28776663/) and
  [Rainforth and Laurenson, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23551415/).
- Progress feedback shows a small average benefit with important variation
  across instruments and settings:
  [de Jong et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33721605/).
- A multicomponent memory-support intervention added to cognitive therapy for
  depression improved recall, but does not isolate closing summaries or show
  the same effect in AI conversations:
  [Dong et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35963181/).
- A review of between-session work supports collaboration, flexibility,
  rationale, alignment with the client's takeaways, and written task summaries,
  mainly in CBT evidence:
  [Kazantzis et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37104804/).

## Record-structure provenance

- SOAP and the problem-oriented medical record have a historical antecedent in
  Lawrence L. Weed's “Medical Records That Guide and Teach”:
  [DOI 10.1056/NEJM196803212781204](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJM196803212781204).
- The 5P categories appear in an NHS mental-health formulation aid:
  [CNTW NHS Foundation Trust 5Ps resource](https://www.cntw.nhs.uk/resources/understanding-what-influences-your-mental-health-and-wellbeing-5ps).
- PICO or PECO questions and evidence-certainty language draw on GRADE, without
  claiming that the journal performs a formal GRADE appraisal:
  [GRADE Book](https://book.gradepro.org/).
- SBAR is an adaptable structured communication framework. The plugin's
  handoff is a non-clinical adaptation and requires independent assessment:
  [AHRQ TeamSTEPPS SBAR](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/sbar.html).
- Historical APA record-keeping guidance was archived in August 2019. It is
  context, not current law or active APA policy:
  [APA Record Keeping Guidelines](https://www.apa.org/practice/guidelines/record-keeping).

## Limits

Recognition of a method or documentation structure does not establish
effectiveness, authorize untrained delivery, or make the journal a clinical
record. The exact session order, documentation cadence, and file fields are
project conventions.

## Update triggers

- Replacement or withdrawal of method or shared-decision guidance.
- Material evidence changing the limits of formulation or progress feedback.
- A change to any clinical-looking schema or its mandatory non-clinical notice.
- New evidence that a selected method family presents a material AI-specific
  risk.
