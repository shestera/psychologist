# Psychometric Instrument Registry

Read this reference before selecting or interpreting a screening or monitoring
measure. It is a dated research shortcut, not a default battery, diagnostic
system, or copy of any questionnaire. Use a measure only when its result could
change a decision and the user agrees to its purpose and burden.

Do not reproduce instrument items or scoring keys from this registry. Obtain
the current official version, verify the exact language and population, follow
its current terms, and record the source and check date in `assessment-plan.md`.
Translation availability does not establish validation. A score never replaces
clinical assessment, direct safety inquiry, or the user's account.

### WHO-5 Well-Being Index

- **Purpose:** brief self-report monitoring of mental well-being over the past
  two weeks; higher scores indicate better well-being.
- **Population:** WHO presents it as a general self-report instrument but does
  not make every population interchangeable. Verify evidence for the user's
  age, setting, and intended decision.
- **Language:** WHO provides more than 30 translations. Use the official
  language file and separately verify population-specific validation.
- **Rights:** WHO lists the instrument under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Preserve the
  attribution and license conditions; do not assume permission for a
  commercial derivative.
- **Limits:** measures positive well-being, not a diagnosis. A low score can
  justify further assessment but does not identify a disorder or cause.
- **Primary source:** [WHO-5 instrument page](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01).

### PHQ-9

- **Purpose:** self-report screening and severity monitoring for depressive
  symptoms; do not present it as a standalone diagnostic interview.
- **Population:** the original study included 6,000 patients from United States
  primary-care and obstetrics-gynaecology clinics, with an independent mental
  health interview in a subset. Verify use in other ages, settings, and groups.
- **Language:** use a current version from PHQ Screeners. Availability of a
  translated form does not by itself establish validity for the current user.
- **Rights:** Pfizer states that content on the PHQ Screeners site is exempt
  from its general copyright restrictions and is free for download and use
  under the site's terms. Recheck those terms before redistribution or a new
  delivery format.
- **Limits:** self-report requires contextual verification. Neither the total
  score nor an individual response establishes depression or suicide risk; any
  current self-harm concern requires direct safety assessment.
- **Primary sources:** [PHQ Screeners terms](https://www.phqscreeners.com/terms)
  and [Kroenke et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11556941/).

### GAD-7

- **Purpose:** brief self-report screening and severity monitoring for
  generalized-anxiety symptoms.
- **Population:** the original criterion study included 2,740 adults from 15
  United States primary-care clinics, with 965 independent mental health
  interviews. Verify fit outside that population and setting.
- **Language:** use a current version from PHQ Screeners and verify the exact
  translation in the intended population.
- **Rights:** the same PHQ Screeners terms apply as for PHQ-9; confirm them at
  the time of use or redistribution.
- **Limits:** anxiety symptoms are transdiagnostic. The score does not establish
  generalized anxiety disorder, exclude medical or substance-related causes,
  or select an intervention.
- **Primary sources:** [PHQ Screeners terms](https://www.phqscreeners.com/terms)
  and [Spitzer et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16717171/).

### AUDIT

- **Purpose:** WHO screening for hazardous and harmful alcohol consumption and
  brief assessment of possible dependence and alcohol-related consequences,
  originally designed for primary health care.
- **Population:** primary-care use is the documented starting setting. Verify
  thresholds and performance for the current population, country, and purpose.
- **Language:** WHO currently links official English, Spanish, Hindi, Japanese,
  and Thai versions. Do not translate or adapt one informally.
- **Rights:** WHO lists the manual under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO. Follow the
  attribution, noncommercial, and share-alike conditions for any permitted
  reuse.
- **Limits:** a score is not a diagnosis or a complete intervention. Possible
  dependence, withdrawal, intoxication, pregnancy, or medical risk requires an
  appropriately qualified clinician and the skill's safety rules.
- **Primary source:** [WHO AUDIT manual and language versions](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-MSD-MSB-01.6a).

## Instruments not pre-cleared

No sexuality-specific questionnaire is pre-cleared by this registry. Before
using one, verify the current rights holder, administration and scoring terms,
validated language and population, clinical interpretation, and whether the
result can change a decision. Do not infer pathology from rarity, identity,
fantasy content, stigma, or a proprietary score.
