# Sexual Health and Safeguarding

## Decisions supported

- Treat HIV, STIs, chemsex, medication effects, and sexualized substance use
  without moralizing while preserving current medical triage.
- Explain U=U only after current official verification and keep it specific to
  sexual HIV transmission.
- Offer high-level chemsex harm reduction without dosing, mixing, sourcing, or
  injection instructions.
- Remain useful for higher-risk consensual kink at a general harm-reduction
  level without technical coaching or claims that precautions remove risk.
- Minimize durable records for disclosures involving people unable to consent
  and route toward verified confidential prevention and safeguarding support.

## Evidence snapshot

- WHO recommends a comprehensive, nonjudgmental, person-centred response to
  chemsex with integrated sexual-health, mental-health, and substance-use
  services:
  [WHO stimulant-drug technical guide](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/hq-hiv-hepatitis-and-stis-library/hiv-prevention-for-people-who-use-stimulant-drugs-2019.pdf)
  and [WHO key-populations guidance](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240052390).
- CDC guidance states that maintained viral suppression prevents sexual
  transmission of HIV. U=U does not replace individualized care for other
  routes, infections, or reproductive decisions:
  [CDC U=U guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/global-hiv-tb/php/our-approach/undetectable-untransmittable.html)
  and [CDC treatment-as-prevention guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/hivpartners/php/hiv-treatment/index.html).
- The broader consent, sexual-health, violence, and safeguarding basis is
  documented in [foundations-and-methods.md](foundations-and-methods.md) and
  [safety-and-stabilization.md](../psychology/safety-and-stabilization.md).

## Operational resources retained in runtime

- AASECT and KAP are discovery starting points, not endorsements. KAP states
  that listed professionals are not verified or screened by NCSF.
- Stop It Now international resources are a starting point for locating
  confidential non-offending support; service confidentiality and reporting
  limits require jurisdiction-specific verification.
- Medical, infection, exposure, reproductive, legal, and directory claims are
  time-sensitive and must be checked at use time.

## Limits

Public-health and clinical guidance does not authorize an AI to provide drug-use
technique, medical treatment, diagnosis, legal advice, or a guarantee of
confidentiality. Evidence specific to AI-delivered sexology support is limited.

## Update triggers

- Changes to U=U, HIV/STI, chemsex, or sexual-health guidance.
- Changes to KAP, AASECT, Stop It Now, or other operational directories.
- New evidence or warnings related to kink harm reduction, non-offending
  services, or durable-record risks.
