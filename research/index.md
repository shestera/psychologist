## Update triggers

Review an entry earlier than its date when a supporting guideline is withdrawn
or replaced, a safety warning changes, an instrument's rights or official
version changes, a material review contradicts the project basis, an
operational directory or service moves, or a shipped instruction changes its
scope. Mark an entry `needs review` rather than silently treating an expired or
conflicted basis as current.
