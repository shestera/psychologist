# Working Formulation — 5P

## Presenting

Current concerns, functional effects, priorities, and user-defined goals.

## Predisposing

Relevant background supported by available information. Keep uncertainty and
possible alternative explanations explicit.

## Precipitating

Events or changes temporally associated with the current concern. Do not infer
causality from sequence alone.

## Perpetuating

Current loops or conditions that may maintain the difficulty, with evidence,
counterevidence, and ways to distinguish alternatives.

## Protective

Strengths, supportive relationships, effective coping, values, access to help,
and other resources.
